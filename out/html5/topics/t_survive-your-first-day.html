Survive your first day

Collect basic materials, craft essential tools, and secure shelter before nightfall.

In Survival with hostile mobs enabled, night is dangerous without light or shelter. Aim to have tools, food, and a safe place before dusk.

  1. Punch a tree to collect logs. Open your inventory and craft logs into planks, then planks into sticks.
  2. On a crafting table (four planks in a 2×2 grid in inventory, then place the table), craft a wooden pickaxe: three planks across the top row and two sticks down the middle column.
  3. Mine stone with the pickaxe and craft a stone pickaxe, then a sword if you expect combat.
  4. Gather coal or burn logs in a furnace to make charcoal for torches (stick below coal or charcoal).
  5. Build a small enclosed shelter or dig into a hillside. Place torches inside so mobs do not spawn there.
  6. Optional: craft a bed from three wool and three planks. Sleeping at night skips to dawn if no hostile mobs are nearby.

After the first night, expand your base, explore for better ores, and experiment with farming and animal breeding for steady food.
